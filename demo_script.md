## SEGMENT 4 — Ablation Result with Traceability [3:15–4:45]

**Total time: 90 seconds**

---

### 4a. Open ablation_results.json [3:15–3:35]

**Screen:** Open `ablations/ablation_results.json` in VS Code

**Say:**
> "Here are the ablation results. Delta A — trained model versus Week 10 baseline on the held-out partition — is 0.00, with a 95% confidence interval of zero to zero and a p-value of 1.0. Delta B — trained versus prompt-engineered on the same backbone — is also 0.00. Both are not significant."

**Action:** Point to `delta_a.delta`, `delta_a.ci_95`, `delta_a.p_value` fields in the JSON.

---

### 4b. Open held_out_traces.jsonl [3:35–4:05]

**Screen:** Open `ablations/held_out_traces.jsonl` in VS Code (or in terminal with `cat`)

**Say:**
> "These are the per-task held-out traces that produced those numbers. There are three tasks — this is the raw scoring output for each condition."

**Action:** Point to one trace entry. Show the `task_id`, `baseline_score`, `trained_score`, and `prompted_score` fields.

**Say:**
> "I'll trace one numeric claim. The Delta A mean score of 0.5315 comes from averaging these three task scores: this first task scored 0.5315, the second 0.5315, the third 0.5315 — all identical. The mean is 0.5315, the delta is zero."

**Action:** Point to each `trained_score` value in the three trace entries.

---

### 4c. Explain the statistics [4:05–4:25]

**Say:**
> "The bootstrap test ran 10,000 resamples over these three scores. Because all three conditions produce identical scores, every resample produces the same delta of zero — hence CI equals zero to zero and p equals 1.0. This is a measurement artefact of n=3 with metadata auto-pass on two of the three tasks, not a pipeline failure. The root cause is documented in the methodology."

---

### 4d. Sign-neutral acknowledgment [4:25–4:45]

**Say:**
> "This is a null result and it is reported honestly. The adapter did produce one measurable change — output length dropped 18% from 256 to 210 words on average — which is visible in the cost-pareto field of the traces. The full diagnosis is in model_card.md."

**Action:** Briefly point to the `cost_pareto` block in one trace entry if visible, or show the `methodology.md` ablation findings section.
